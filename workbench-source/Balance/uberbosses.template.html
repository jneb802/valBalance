Uber bosses

Every tunable number for the Uber boss attacks and encounter rules. Vanilla attacks are shown for reference and cannot be changed here.

Local draft
How these values reach the game. Export this file and commit it as Balance/uberbosses.json. The Unity authoring script bakes the attack values into the Uber Bosses bundle, and apply_uberbosses.py writes the encounter settings into Config/encounters.yaml. Nothing is deployed from this page.

Base health is written to Config/encounters.yaml with the other settings and sets the boss's health before star scaling. TTK is the median of 96 simulated fights with the player's primary attack, no misses and no boss abilities. HTD counts hits of that one kind from full player health, with no healing or blocking. Damage per hit and hit counts below are the same numbers the Unity authoring bakes into the bundle; vanilla weapons stay read-only because the mod does not change them.
