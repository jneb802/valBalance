Monster scaling assumptions

Each creature starts with its recorded resolved values. Enter an override to apply it to all creatures. Empty fields use each creature's default.

Health = base health × (health base + stars × health per star). Attack damage = base attack × damage base × (1 + stars × damage per star). Independent area effects keep their own damage.

Time to kill uses primary attacks and stamina pauses, without misses, movement, blocking or healing. Hits to die uses the most damaging recorded attack. These are planning estimates. Scaling data is the recorded October 17, 2026 configuration; it is not a current game-data scan. Imported maximum health and stamina require game values. Weapon and armor support is limited to verified source records.
