Shrine Balance

Player strength → creature roles → wave composition

Planning estimates, not measured combat results. Snuffiss defenses are prefilled. Player hit damage, attack timings, uptime, and enemy contact rates are example inputs. Replace them with observations before tuning a live event.

1 · Player

What these inputs mean

Effective hit damage means immediate health removed from the target after its defenses. Use an enemy-specific override when resistances differ. Do not put the earlier 791 mixed poison payload here. Attack interval is time between successful hits during uninterrupted attacks. First-hit delay is time from attack start to its first impact.

Attack uptime approximates time available to attack during combat. Healing is a manually estimated sustained recovery rate for wave pressure only. It is not calculated from lifesteal gear. Snuffiss health uses the intended modifier order (672.45); the unresolved alternative is 646.24.

3 · Creature results

HTK = hits to kill the creature. HTD = hits to kill the player. HTD uses unblocked hits, changing armor at the health threshold, with no healing or random avoidance. TTD starts at the first incoming hit. A lethal first hit has TTD 0.

4 · Wave composition

Set the number of each creature in each wave. Contact % estimates how many enemy attack opportunities reach the player; it combines movement and successful defense into a coarse assumption.

Model limits

Wave clear time assumes sequential single-target kills, a fresh first-hit delay per target, and the entered attack uptime. It excludes area damage, poison, chain lightning, shaman healing, travel, and spawn delays. It is not a forecast of actual wave duration.

Wave TTD holds the whole pack alive and spreads incoming damage evenly over time, subtracting the entered healing rate. Armor changes at the health threshold. This shows starting pressure; it does not model enemies dying, simultaneous hits, stagger, shields, parries, conditional hit avoidance, or reactive healing. A “sustain holds” result does not mean burst damage is safe.

The clear-time / TTD ratio is a comparison of these assumptions, not a difficulty score. Use live tests to check damage taken, recovery, deaths, and actual wave duration. Scenarios save in this browser and can be exported as JSON. This app does not generate Fortress configuration.
